Our Department

About EECP Computer Science

The EECP Computer Science Department focuses on developing students' knowledge and practical skills in computer science, programming, software development, and information technology.

Through academic instruction, hands-on activities, collaborative projects, and technology-based learning, students are encouraged to strengthen their logical thinking, creativity, communication, and problem-solving abilities.

The department promotes responsible technology use, teamwork, continuous learning, and ethical practices in the digital environment.

Department Background

The department is committed to providing quality education and developing competent, ethical, and innovative computing professionals.
